Fix Yellow Screen

Betulkan Warna Kuning (Yellowish) di OBS Fix Yellowish Screen in OBS

Masalah video nampak kuning atau pucat adalah perkara biasa di masjid kerana cahaya lampu kalimantang. Jangan risau, ia boleh dibetulkan dalam OBS tanpa perlu beli kamera baru!

Yellowish or pale video is common in mosques due to fluorescent lighting. Don't worry, it can be fixed in OBS without buying a new camera!

Nota: Panduan ini menggunakan fungsi Filters > Color Correction dalam OBS. Note: This guide uses the Filters > Color Correction function in OBS.

Langkah Demi LangkahStep by Step

1 Pilih Sumber KameraSelect Camera Source

Di panel Sources (bawah kiri OBS), klik pada sumber Video Capture Device (kamera masjid Tuan). In the Sources panel (bottom left of OBS), click on your Video Capture Device source (mosque camera).

2 Buka Menu FiltersOpen Filters Menu

Klik butang Filters di bahagian atas panel Sources (sebelah butang Properties). Click the Filters button at the top of the Sources panel (next to Properties).

3 Tambah Color CorrectionAdd Color Correction

Di tetingkap Filters, klik butang + di bawah kiri. Pilih Color Correction dari senarai. In the Filters window, click the + button at the bottom left. Select Color Correction from the list.

4 Laraskan WarnaAdjust the Colors

Sekarang Tuan akan nampak beberapa slider. Laraskan seperti panduan di bawah: Now you will see several sliders. Adjust them as below:

  • Gamma: Turunkan sedikit (contoh: dari 1.0 ke 0.9) untuk kurangkan kecerahan melampau.Gamma: Lower slightly (e.g., from 1.0 to 0.9) to reduce overexposure.
  • Saturation: Turunkan sedikit (contoh: dari 1.0 ke 0.8) untuk kurangkan warna kuning yang terlalu "terang".Saturation: Lower slightly (e.g., from 1.0 to 0.8) to reduce the overly "bright" yellow.
  • Color Multiply: (Ini paling penting) Tambah warna Biru (Blue) atau Cyan sedikit untuk mengimbangi warna kuning. Klik pada kotak warna, pilih biru muda, dan naikkan sedikit.Color Multiply: (Most important) Add a little Blue or Cyan to balance the yellow. Click the color box, select light blue, and increase slightly.

Panduan Tetapan (Contoh)Settings Guide (Example)

Berikut adalah tetapan yang biasa digunakan untuk membetulkan warna kuning di masjid. Tuan boleh cuba nilai ini dan laraskan mengikut kesesuaian.

These are common settings to fix yellow in mosques. You can try these values and adjust to your preference.

TetapanSetting Nilai DisyorkanRecommended Value
Gamma 0.80 - 0.90
Saturation 0.70 - 0.90
Brightness 0.00 (tidak ubah)
Contrast 1.00 - 1.10
Color Multiply Light Blue / Cyan
Tip: Jangan terus ubah nilai. Buat secara perlahan dan tengok kesan pada skrin OBS. Tip: Don't change values abruptly. Do it slowly and observe the effect on the OBS screen.

Tips TambahanExtra Tips

  • White Balance Kamera: Jika kamera Tuan ada tetapan White Balance (WB), cuba tukar dari Auto ke Manual dan pilih mod "Fluorescent" atau "Indoor".Camera White Balance: If your camera has White Balance (WB) settings, try switching from Auto to Manual and select "Fluorescent" or "Indoor" mode.
  • Lampu Kuning: Elakkan meletakkan kamera terlalu dekat dengan lampu kuning di masjid. Cahaya ini yang menyebabkan warna kuning.Yellow Lights: Avoid placing the camera too close to yellow lights in the mosque. This is the main cause of the yellow color.
  • Ujian Stream: Selepas membetulkan warna, buat "Test Stream" atau "Start Recording" sekejap untuk pastikan warna nampak elok di Facebook.Test Stream: After correcting colors, do a "Test Stream" or "Start Recording" briefly to ensure colors look good on Facebook.
Amaran: Jangan lupa klik OK atau tutup tetingkap Filters selepas selesai. Jika tidak, tetapan mungkin tidak disimpan. Warning: Don't forget to click OK or close the Filters window after you finish. Otherwise, settings may not be saved.
