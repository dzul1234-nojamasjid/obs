OBS Bitrate Guide

1. Apa Itu Bitrate? 1. What is Bitrate?

Bitrate adalah jumlah data video yang dihantar ke Facebook setiap detik. Ia diukur dalam kbps (kilobit per saat).

Bitrate is the amount of video data sent to Facebook every second. It is measured in kbps (kilobits per second).

Bayangkan seperti paip air:Imagine it like a water pipe:

  • Bitrate Rendah: Paip kecil. Air (data) sikit. Gambar nampak kabur/pecah (pixelated).Low Bitrate: Small pipe. Little water (data). Image looks blurry/pixelated.
  • Bitrate Tinggi: Paip besar. Air banyak. Gambar tajam dan jelas. Tapi perlu internet laju!High Bitrate: Large pipe. Plenty of water. Image is sharp and clear. But needs fast internet!
Peraturan Emas: Jika bitrate terlalu tinggi untuk internet Tuan, siaran akan terputus-putus (buffering/lag). Jika terlalu rendah, gambar akan kabur. Golden Rule: If the bitrate is too high for your internet, the stream will buffer/lag. If too low, the image will be blurry.

2. Tetapan Bitrate Yang Disyorkan2. Recommended Bitrate Settings

Pilih bitrate berdasarkan kelajuan upload internet di masjid Tuan. Jangan letak lebih tinggi daripada kemampuan internet.

Choose a bitrate based on the internet upload speed at your mosque. Don't set it higher than your internet capability.

Disyorkan untuk Masjid: Gunakan 1080p pada 4000 kbps. Ia memberi kualiti yang sangat baik tanpa membebankan internet. Recommended for Mosque: Use 1080p at 4000 kbps. It gives excellent quality without straining the internet.

3. Cara Tukar Bitrate di OBS3. How to Change Bitrate in OBS

1 Buka SettingsOpen Settings

Klik butang Settings di penjuru kanan bawah OBS. Click the Settings button at the bottom right of OBS.

2 Pergi ke OutputGo to Output

Di menu kiri, klik Output. Pastikan Output Mode ditukar kepada Advanced. In the left menu, click Output. Make sure Output Mode is set to Advanced.

3 Pilih Tab StreamingSelect Streaming Tab

Klik tab Streaming. Cari kotak Bitrate. Click the Streaming tab. Find the Bitrate box.

4 Masukkan Nilai BitrateEnter Bitrate Value

Taip nilai yang disyorkan (contoh: 4000). Pastikan kotak di sebelahnya tertulis Kbps. Type the recommended value (e.g., 4000). Make sure the box next to it says Kbps.

5 SimpanSave

Klik Apply dan kemudian OK. Click Apply and then OK.

4. Masalah & Penyelesaian4. Issues & Solutions

Masalah A: Siaran Terputus-putus / LagProblem A: Stream Buffering / Lag

  • Punca: Bitrate terlalu tinggi untuk internet Tuan.Cause: Bitrate too high for your internet.
  • Penyelesaian: Turunkan Bitrate. Contoh: Dari 4000 ke 3000 kbps. Atau turunkan resolusi dari 1080p ke 720p.Solution: Lower the Bitrate. E.g., From 4000 to 3000 kbps. Or lower resolution from 1080p to 720p.

Masalah B: Gambar Nampak Kabur / Pecah (Pixelated)Problem B: Image Looks Blurry / Pixelated

  • Punca: Bitrate terlalu rendah.Cause: Bitrate too low.
  • Penyelesaian: Naikkan Bitrate. Contoh: Dari 2500 ke 4000 kbps. Pastikan internet Tuan mampu.Solution: Increase Bitrate. E.g., From 2500 to 4000 kbps. Ensure your internet can handle it.

Masalah C: Facebook Beri Amaran "Kualiti Rendah"Problem C: Facebook Warns "Low Quality"

  • Punca: Facebook mengesan bitrate Tuan tidak stabil.Cause: Facebook detects your bitrate is unstable.
  • Penyelesaian: Semak kelajuan internet Tuan. Gunakan kabel LAN (bukan WiFi) untuk sambungan yang lebih stabil.Solution: Check your internet speed. Use a LAN cable (not WiFi) for a more stable connection.
Penting: Jangan sesekali letak bitrate melebihi 50% daripada kelajuan upload internet Tuan. Contoh: Jika upload 10 Mbps, jangan letak bitrate lebih dari 5000 kbps. Important: Never set bitrate more than 50% of your internet upload speed. E.g., If upload is 10 Mbps, don't set bitrate higher than 5000 kbps.
